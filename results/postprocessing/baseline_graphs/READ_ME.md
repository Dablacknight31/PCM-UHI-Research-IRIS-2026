# Saved baseline comparisons

These CSVs contain the three-material baseline comparison, daily response, monthly gross cooling/warming, screening and chart-source rows. Source experiments are in [accepted baselines](../../category3/accepted_baselines). Final original graphics are listed in the [paper figure index](../../../PAPER_FIGURE_INDEX.md).

Mean annual spatial anomalies use mK (0.001 K); peak anomalies use their own scale. A single coverage case is not a completed coverage-sensitivity assessment. [Scientific provenance](../../../docs/scientific_provenance.md) explains source identities and privately retained dependencies.
