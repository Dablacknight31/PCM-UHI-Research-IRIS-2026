# Limits and missing scientific evidence

- Seven included figures lack exact generating code. Heat-diversion and one-way indoor illustrations also lack original numerical exports and scenario configurations.
- A full hourly atmospheric spatial cube and exact within-hour extrema times are unavailable. Compact saved fields cannot reconstruct them.
- The full uncertainty/feedback ensemble, building-scale indoor model, measured field validation and deployment evidence are incomplete.
- The exact compiled PCM workbook and GAUL-conditioned preprocessing inputs are not distributed. Source-property conflicts remain unresolved.
- The scientific workflow has not been independently rerun for a full year from the public package. Pinned dependency installation on new platforms is unverified.

See [limitations](limitations.md), [reproducibility](../REPRODUCIBILITY.md), [figure index](../PAPER_FIGURE_INDEX.md), and [data availability](../DATA_AVAILABILITY.md).
