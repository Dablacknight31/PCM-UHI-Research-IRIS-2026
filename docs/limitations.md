# Scientific and evidence limitations

- **Scope:** one selected Delhi hotspot, three screened property sets and one weather year. The atmospheric domain is larger than the treated hotspot; this is not demonstrated citywide UHI mitigation.
- **Material evidence:** executed and source sheets conflict for lauric acid, OM42 and PEG1000. “USER_VERIFIED” and evidence-tier labels in recorded scientific metadata are not independent thermophysical validation. No inputs were retrospectively corrected.
- **Roof/control:** homogeneous exposed PCM with insulated lower boundary; no complete building assembly or experimentally validated conventional/cool-roof comparator. Control means latent heat alone is zero.
- **Atmosphere:** prescribed vertically averaged 2D advection–diffusion with fixed effective mixing height and simplified wind/exchange. No urban CFD, vertical temperature gradients or measured atmospheric validation.
- **Heat timing:** cooling and warming both occur. Positive annual sensible-flux differences limit claims of permanent heat removal; signed near-zero air means can conceal gross responses.
- **Incomplete experiments:** wider cell-195 workflow and later resume failed; atmospheric sensitivity/feedback ensemble and deployment assessment are incomplete. Three accepted saved cases do not prove complete pipeline success.
- **Reproduction:** Saved-data relationships and public packaging are verified; a full independent annual rerun is not established. Authorized remote acquisition and exact non-distributed inputs are required for deliberate fresh execution.
- **Spatial evidence:** compact annual and synchronous event fields exist; arbitrary hourly maps, exact peak substep times and a full hourly cube are absent.
- **Figure provenance:** seven later graphics lack exact original generators. Heat-diversion/indoor exports are absent. The included original images are preserved; exact regeneration is not established.
- **Exploratory scope:** imposed diversion fractions do not establish a heat exchanger's performance. One-way indoor illustration does not establish indoor temperature or building energy savings. No missing experiment was reconstructed.
- **Deployment:** building eligibility, containment, leakage, fire, corrosion, weather exposure, structural load and complete assembly evidence are insufficient. Active mass excludes installation components.
- **Release:** intact PCM/source workbooks and GAUL-dependent assets are withheld from the candidate. A public clone cannot reproduce every stage unaided. The marked-up PDF is local only; clean final paper not supplied. No selected project-wide license grants broad reuse.

The supported conclusion is conditional rooftop and hotspot air cooling with compensating warming under the recorded model, not verified deployment performance.
